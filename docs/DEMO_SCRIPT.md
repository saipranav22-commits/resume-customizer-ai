> *Meanwhile, modern candidates who turn to generic LLMs like ChatGPT often end up with fabricated credentials, invented degrees, or hallucinated tools that fail technical background checks.*
>

### Scene 3: Calibrated Dual Scoring & Gap Analysis (1:00 – 1:40)

**Visual Setup**:
- The screen transitions seamlessly into the **Studio Workspace** and displays the **ATS Optimization Dashboard**.

**On-Screen Actions**:
- Hover cursor over **Score A: Job Description Match Gauge (e.g., 88%)**.
- Highlight the 6-factor breakdown: Required Skills (35%), Experience Alignment (20%), Responsibilities (15%), Preferred Skills (15%), Domain Keywords (10%), and Formatting (5%).
- Hover cursor over **Score B: ATS Document Quality Audit Gauge (e.g., 94%)**.
- Highlight the 5 document health factors: Parseability, Section Structure, Readability, Consistency, and Contact Completeness.
- Scroll down to the **Advisory Missing Keywords & Gap Analysis** card. Point to missing skills such as *Terraform* and *Kubernetes*.

**Voiceover Script**:
> *"Here is our calibrated dual scoring engine. Unlike naive tools that display arbitrary 100% scores, our engine uses rigorous mathematical weighting.*
> 
> *Score A measures contextual Job Match across six weighted factors, prioritizing core technical competencies and seniority.*
> 
> *Score B measures technical ATS Parseability across five structural metrics — validating standard headings, bullet length economy, and contact completeness.*
> 
> *Notice this dedicated Gap Analysis panel: skills from the JD that Alex didn't list — like Terraform — are isolated here as learning opportunities. They are never injected into the candidate's resume without their consent."*

**Visual Setup**:

- Hover over the updated bullet and point out the repeatable **"Improve Again"** feature.
- Click **"Re-check Score"** in the Command Bar.

**Voiceover Script**:
> *"Now let's examine our continuous studio canvas. This isn't a static markdown preview — it is a fully interactive, in-place document editor.*
> 
> *When you hover over any achievement bullet, our contextual 'AI Improve' tool offers instant, single-bullet enhancements with keyword diffs.*
> 
> *Watch this: after accepting an enhancement, you can immediately trigger 'Improve Again' to iteratively hone the bullet. When we hit 'Re-check Score', the backend recalculates both Score A and Score B against the live modified draft in real time."*

## 💡 Presenter Best Practices & Fallback Plan

1. **Audio Quality**: Use a high-quality USB condenser microphone with zero background noise.
2. **Display Resolution**: Record at 1920x1080 (1080p) at 60 FPS with browser zoom set to 100%.
3. **Network Resilience**: In case of transient Wi-Fi drops, the application's built-in **Deterministic Heuristic Fallback Engine** ensures that all tailoring, scoring, and PDF generation steps complete flawlessly without external network dependencies.
4. **Data Privacy**: Use strictly the pre-loaded synthetic profile for **Alex Morgan**. Never input personal telephone numbers or actual client credentials during a public presentation.
