---

### 🛡️ Ethical Anti-Fabrication Standards

1. **Verification-Only Alignment**: System prompts strictly limit skills and bullet claims to facts present in the uploaded document.
2. **Transparent Gap Isolation**: Any skill in the target JD not verified on the resume is segregated into an advisory "Missing Keywords" panel and is NEVER injected into candidate credentials.
3. **Actionable Guidance**: Candidates receive recommendations on how to acquire or demonstrate missing skills through portfolio projects, coursework, or cover letters.
